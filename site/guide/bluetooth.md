# Bluetooth

The **Bluetooth** page of Isotope System pairs and connects headphones, speakers, keyboards, mice and other devices. Every user can use it.

Open the page: press [[Super+Escape]] and choose **Bluetooth**. On niri, this entry opens the Bluetooth panel of the control center; choose **System**, then **Bluetooth** for the page.

## Pair a device

1. If the page says **Bluetooth is off**, click **Turn on**.
2. Put the device in pairing mode. The manual of the device tells you how.
3. Click **Search for devices**. The search runs for 30 seconds.
4. Find the device and click **Pair**.
5. If the page asks for a code, do what it tells you:
   compare the code on the device and confirm, type the code on the device, or type the PIN of the device.

The device is then paired and connects.

## Connect, disconnect and forget

Next to a paired device:

- Click **Connect** or **Disconnect**.
- Click **Forget**, then confirm, to remove the device from this computer. To use it again, pair it again.

The page shows the battery level of a device when the device tells it.

## Choose the sound quality

For headphones and speakers, choose the codec under **Sound quality**. A higher quality uses more battery and needs a good signal.

## Send a file

1. Next to a paired phone or computer, click **Send file…**.
2. Choose the file.
3. Accept the file on the other device.

## Receive a file

When another device sends you a file, a window asks if you accept it. If you do not answer in 50 seconds, Isotope refuses the file. Isotope saves the files that you accept in your **Downloads** folder.

## The quick menus

- On Sway and i3, [[Super+Shift+B]] opens a quick Bluetooth menu. Its **Settings…** item opens the Bluetooth page.
- On COSMIC, the Bluetooth row of the quick settings turns Bluetooth on and off.
- On niri, use the control center: [[Super+S]].

## If there is no adapter

If the page says **No Bluetooth adapter**, the computer has no Bluetooth, or a key or a switch turned it off. Look for a wireless key on the keyboard, and look in the firmware setup.
